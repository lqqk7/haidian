「雨天断点」三类成因 → 指向哪一件设施The three causes of a wet-weather break and the feature each points to

积水Ponding

指向该段滞留带容积与溢流标高、通行面横坡方向——三者均为现场核定条件，读数产出后按段回填。Points to that segment's retention volume and overflow level and to the cross-fall direction — all site-verification conditions.

湿滑Slip surface

指向铺装材料判据（透水防滑、平整连续、反光受控）；断点一经登记即为该段判据未成立的现场证据。Points to the paving criterion — permeable, slip-resistant, level, continuous, glare-controlled. A registered break is site evidence.

排水口阻挡轮椅Grate blocking a wheelchair

指向线性排水沟位置与盖板方向，以及滞留带与通行面之间的可探知边界；与触觉带读数同处合并登记。Points to the channel's position and grating orientation and to the detectable edge; joined with the tactile reading, never double counted.

六项空间审计读数当前全部为零值。本对应关系不产出读数，只答「断点之后该看哪一件设施」。All six spatial-audit readings are currently zero. The correspondence produces no reading: it says which feature to inspect, never how many breaks there ought to be.
